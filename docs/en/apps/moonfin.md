### Good to know { #samsung-tv-good-to-know }

!!! warning "For advanced users only"

    Moonfin is not in the Samsung app store yet, so you have to install it manually (sideload it).
    This needs Developer Mode turned on on your TV. Try it at your own risk. We expect Moonfin to
    come to the Samsung app store later. Until then, we recommend
    [Jellyfin for Samsung TV](jellyfin-samsung-tv.md#samsung-tv).
