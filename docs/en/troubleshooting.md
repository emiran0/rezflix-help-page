# Troubleshooting

Something not working? Find your problem below and try the steps in order, simplest first. Most
problems are fixed within the first two or three steps.

## Try these first { #try-these-first }

These quick checks fix many problems, whatever app or device you use.

1. Close the app completely and open it again. In a browser, reload the page.
2. Try a different movie or show. If only one title has the problem, note its name for us.
3. Check your internet. Open another website or app and see if it loads normally.
4. Update your app to the latest version.
5. Restart your device. On a TV or streaming device, turn it off at the power, wait a few
   seconds, and turn it on again.

!!! tip "Quick test: try Rezflix Web"

    Open the same title in [Rezflix Web](https://watch.rezflixtv.com){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }
    in a browser on a computer or phone.

    - **It works there:** the problem is your app or device. The steps below help with that.
    - **It does not work there either:** the problem is probably on our side.
      [Contact us](contact.md) and tell us which title it is.

## Playback

!!! info "Why videos get slow, blurry, or freeze"

    Rezflix can play a video in two ways:

    - **Direct play:** your device plays the original file as it is. This gives the best picture
      and the smoothest playback.
    - **Converting (transcoding):** our server converts the video while you watch, because your
      app or one of its settings does not accept the original file. This can lower the quality,
      make the video slow to start, and cause freezes.

    Most slow, blurry, or freezing videos are being converted. The steps below help your device
    play the original file instead.

### Video will not play or shows an error { #video-will-not-play-or-shows-an-error }

The video does not start, stays on a black screen, or shows an error message.

**Try this**

1. Write down or take a photo of the error message, if there is one.
2. Do the [quick checks](#try-these-first) above.
3. Make sure your app is up to date. Old versions of some apps can stop working after Rezflix is
   updated.
4. Open the player settings and choose a lower quality, then play the title again.
5. Try the same title in [Rezflix Web](https://watch.rezflixtv.com){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }
   or in [another app](apps/index.md).

**Still not working?** [Contact us](contact.md) with [these details](#before-you-contact-us).

### Video keeps buffering or freezing { #video-keeps-buffering-or-freezing }

The video stops to load, freezes, or keeps showing a loading circle.

**Try this**

1. Check your internet. Pause the video and open another website or app. If that is slow too,
   the problem is your connection.
2. Open the quality options in the player. If there is an **Original** or **Direct** option,
   choose it.
3. Open the settings of your app (or of Rezflix Web) and find the network, internet, or bitrate
   setting. Set it to the **maximum**, even if it is on **Auto**. See
   [video quality settings](settings.md#video-quality).
4. Turn subtitles off and see if playback becomes smooth. Some subtitle types make the video
   harder to stream in some apps.
5. If you use Wi-Fi, move your device closer to the router, or connect it with a network cable.
6. Try a [recommended app](apps/index.md) instead of the browser.
7. Only if your internet is slow: choose a lower quality in the player. The video then needs less
   internet speed, but it is converted, so the picture is softer.

!!! tip "Is everything buffering, on every device?"

    If every title buffers on every device, even with fast internet, the problem is probably on
    our side. [Contact us](contact.md).

**Still not working?** [Contact us](contact.md) with [these details](#before-you-contact-us).

### Video quality is low or blurry { #video-quality-is-low-or-blurry }

The picture looks soft, blocky, or lower quality than expected.

**Try this**

1. Open the quality options in the player. If there is an **Original** or **Direct** option,
   choose it. If not, choose the highest quality, even if it is on **Auto**.
2. Open the settings of your app (or of Rezflix Web) and find the network, internet, or bitrate
   setting. Set it to the **maximum**, even if it is on **Auto**. See
   [video quality settings](settings.md#video-quality).
3. Use an app instead of the browser. Browsers cannot play every video format as it is, so the
   video is sometimes converted to a lower quality. See [Browser or app?](quick-start.md#browser-or-app)
4. Check your internet speed. When the connection is slow, the quality can drop to keep the video
   playing.
5. Make sure the title itself is not the problem. Try another movie or show and compare.

**Still not working?** [Contact us](contact.md) and tell us the title. If a movie or show is only
available in low quality, we can look for a better version.

## Subtitles

### Subtitles are not showing { #subtitles-are-not-showing }

You turned subtitles on, but nothing appears on screen.

**Try this**

1. Open the subtitles menu in the player and make sure a subtitle track is selected, not
   **Off**.
2. Pick a different subtitle track in your language. A track marked **Forced** only shows text
   when characters speak another language, so it can look like subtitles are missing.
3. No subtitles in your language? If your app's player has a **search subtitles** option, use
   it to find and download them. Rezflix supports this, but not every app has the option.
4. Stop the video and play it again.
5. Try the same title in [Rezflix Web](https://watch.rezflixtv.com){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }.
   If subtitles show there, try a [different app](apps/index.md) on your device.

**Still not working?** [Contact us](contact.md) with the title and the subtitle language you need.
If a title has no subtitles in your language, let us know and we will try to add them.

### Subtitles are out of sync { #subtitles-are-out-of-sync }

Subtitles appear too early or too late.

**Try this**

1. Pick a different subtitle track in the same language, if there is one. Each track can be timed
   differently.
2. If your app's player has a **search subtitles** option, download another subtitle in the same
   language. A different version is often timed correctly.
3. Stop the video and play it again.
4. If your player has a **subtitle offset** setting, use it to move the subtitles earlier or
   later in small steps until they match.

**Still not working?** [Contact us](contact.md) with the title, the episode, and the subtitle
track name. We can replace badly timed subtitles.

## Audio and language

### No sound, or sound out of sync { #no-sound-or-sound-out-of-sync }

The video plays without sound, or the sound does not match the picture.

**Try this**

1. Check the volume and mute on your device, your TV, and in the player.
2. Open the audio menu in the player and pick a different audio track. Some advanced formats
   (such as Dolby Atmos or DTS) do not play on every device. A track marked **AAC**, **AC3**, or
   **Stereo** works almost everywhere.
3. If the sound is out of sync, pause the video for a few seconds and play it again, or skip back
   10 seconds.
4. Stop the video and play it again.

??? info "Using a soundbar or AV receiver?"

    Try the TV speakers first. If the sound works there, the problem is the connection to your
    soundbar or receiver. Some apps have an audio passthrough (bitstream) setting. If yours does,
    try turning it off.

**Still not working?** [Contact us](contact.md) with the title and the audio track you tried.

### Wrong audio or subtitle language { #wrong-audio-or-subtitle-language }

The movie or show plays in a language you did not expect, or shows subtitles you do not want.

**Try this**

1. Open the audio menu in the player and pick your language.
2. Open the subtitles menu and pick your language, or turn subtitles off.
3. Subtitles in your language are missing? If your app's player has a **search subtitles**
   option, use it to find and download them.
4. To make this automatic for every title, set your preferred audio and subtitle languages in your
   [settings](settings.md#audio-and-subtitle-languages).

**Still not working?** If your audio language is not in the list, the title does not have it yet.
[Contact us](contact.md) and we will see if we can add it.

## Account

### I cannot log in { #i-cannot-log-in }

Rezflix does not accept your username or password, or cannot connect.

**Try this**

1. In an app, check the server address. It must be exactly
   `https://watch.rezflixtv.com`. You can copy it from [Quick start](quick-start.md#rezflix-server-address).
2. Check your password. Passwords are case-sensitive, so check Caps Lock. On phones, watch out
   for an automatic capital letter or a space at the end.
3. Try signing in to [Rezflix Web](https://watch.rezflixtv.com){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }.
   If that works, update your app, or try [another app](apps/index.md).
4. Try [Quick Connect](quick-start.md#sign-in-with-quick-connect) from a device where you are
   already signed in.

!!! warning "Do not keep guessing your password"

    Too many wrong attempts can lock your account. If you are not sure of your password,
    [contact us](contact.md) and we will reset it.

**Still not working?** [Contact us](contact.md). We can reset your password or unlock your
account.

### Quick Connect is not working { #quick-connect-is-not-working }

The code is not accepted, or you cannot find the Quick Connect option.

**Try this**

1. Codes only work for a short time. On the new device, get a new code and enter it right away.
2. Check that you typed the code exactly, all six digits.
3. Enter the code on a device signed in with your own account. The new device signs in as the
   person who enters the code.
4. If your app has no Quick Connect option, sign in with your username and password instead.
   Updating the app may add it.

See [Sign in with Quick Connect](quick-start.md#sign-in-with-quick-connect) for the full steps.

**Still not working?** Sign in with your username and password, or [contact us](contact.md).

## Finding and requesting

### I cannot find a movie or show { #i-cannot-find-a-movie-or-show }

**Try this**

1. Search again with a shorter part of the title, and check the spelling.
2. Try the original title. Some movies and shows are listed under their original name.
3. If it is still not there, Rezflix does not have it yet.
   [Request it](requesting.md) in Rezflix Request.

**Still not working?** If you know a title was added but you cannot find it,
[contact us](contact.md).

### My request is not on Rezflix yet { #my-request-is-not-on-rezflix-yet }

**Try this**

1. Open [Rezflix Request](https://request.rezflixtv.com){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" } and
   check the status of your request:
    - **Pending:** waiting for us to approve it.
    - **Processing:** approved, and we are adding it.
    - **Partially available:** some episodes or seasons are on Rezflix, the rest are coming.
    - **Available:** it is on Rezflix. Search for it, or check the email we sent you.
    - **Declined:** we could not add it this time.
2. Wait a little. Most requests are added quickly, but some titles take longer, for example when
   they are very new.

**Still not working?** If your request has been processing for a long time, [contact us](contact.md)
with the title.

## Before you contact us { #before-you-contact-us }

If nothing here helped, [contact us](contact.md). Include as much of this as you can, so we can
fix it faster:

- The movie or show, and the season and episode
- Your device (for example "Samsung TV" or "iPhone") and the app you use
- What you did, and what happened
- The error message, or a photo of the screen
- Roughly when it happened
- Whether it also happens in [Rezflix Web](https://watch.rezflixtv.com){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }
