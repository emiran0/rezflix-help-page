# Apps

Pick the device you want to watch on. Each device shows the app we recommend, and a link to every
other app that works on it.

??? info "Why are there so many apps?"

    Rezflix runs on Jellyfin, a free and open source media server. Besides the official Jellyfin
    apps, the community builds many others, and some of them work better than the official ones.
    That is why we recommend a specific app for each device.
